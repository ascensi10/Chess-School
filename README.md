### Core Philosophy

The Soviet School does not start with openings.

1.  **Endgame First:** With few pieces, the student learns piece coordination, King activity, Opposition, Zugzwang, and Key Squares.
2.  **Tactics Second:** Calculation is the foundation. Forced lines are studied before motifs.
3.  **Positional Understanding Third:** After calculation is established, piece placement, center control, and pawn structures are studied.
4.  **Opening Last:** The opening is a means to reach a playable middlegame.

### Study Methodology

**Required Protocol:**
- Calculate with eyes before moving.
- After solving, flip the board and solve from the opponent's perspective.
- Solve without hints.
- Complete practical exercise immediately after theoretical lesson.
- Analyze Rapid games (10+0 minimum) without an engine first, then verify with analysis.
